## The order I propose

Ten chapters. The changes from the README's nine are one new chapter and a renumbering, which is
a change to the chapter list and so Michael's to rule.

1. **Why a room.** The night-orders cost and the foreman-wake census, ten weeks apart.
2. **Room norms.** The five rules, linked to working-with-ai's skill, and the four amendments told
   with the misses that caused them; claims and lending a box.
3. **Seats and boxes** (revised). The covenant base and its extensions, charters read by pointer,
   six orders, the agent OS user, seats from a file; the 704-byte error corrected.
4. **One fact, one place** (new). The box layer, foreign rules by proximity, hooks that give
   orders, the grounding check.
5. **Pairing machines.** The PIN dance for boxes and the ssh path for seats.
6. **The shared brain.** The record, the importer, the loader, the shared tree.
7. **Presiding.** Delegation, emergency force with accounting, staffed rather than built.
8. **Fan-out versus shepherd.**
9. **Oracles before armies.** The grounding check, the wall-check gate, three levels of done.
10. **When it breaks.** Incident reports, the second vantage, twins and deaf seats, the dead-man
    timer, hardware tested for correctness.

The box layer goes fourth because a reader needs seats and boxes first, and pairing a box today
begins by writing its `box.md`. The alternative is to fold it into a longer chapter 3 and keep
nine chapters. That makes chapter 3 carry two arguments; I recommend the split.
